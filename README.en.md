# Hi, I'm Isaque Soares 👋

🚀 **Mid-level Developer | Founder | Backend & Software Architecture**  
🇧🇷 Brazil

I have **4 years of experience** in software development and I'm the **founder of Grupo Venture**, a tech company with three branches: **systems** (niche software products), **studio** (visual identity, websites and digital presence) and **assistance** (IT support, networking and infrastructure).

My strength is the **backend**: I build **APIs** and services, mostly with **Python**, and I think through the whole system, from architecture to deployment. For native applications I use **Java** and **.NET**, and when it makes sense I move parts of them to Python to gain speed and save memory.

Right now I'm focused on the shift to **agent-oriented development**: AI orchestration, **RAG** to give models memory and context, and AI as a real part of the engineering workflow, delegating tasks with judgment while keeping control of the architecture.

## 🏢 At Grupo Venture

I design and build the company's products end to end, from architecture to delivery:

- 📦 **Desktop systems in production** for operations management, logistics and financial control
- 🧾 **Tax document system** with a document queue, operator review and audit trail
- 🍽️ **Local-first POS** for restaurants, with networked service via QR Code
- ⚖️ **Hardware integration**: scales, printers and barcode readers
- 🔗 **Client-server architecture** on local networks, with local APIs and automatic backup
- 🤖 **AI virtual assistant**: persistent memory, knowledge base and WhatsApp support
- 🧠 **Local LLMs** (Ollama) integrated into the systems

*These systems are proprietary, so their code is not public.*
